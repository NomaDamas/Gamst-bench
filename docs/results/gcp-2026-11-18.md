## Interpretation limits

These are engineering smoke/dev-subset observations, not evidence that GAMST is superior. The Qwen GAMST FastBench scores are especially affected by the extension/native protocol interaction and should not be generalized. Full held-out runs, stable rate limits, independent audit and session-level uncertainty are still required.
